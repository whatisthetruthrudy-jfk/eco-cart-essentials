Privacy Policy

This privacy policy covers the Lighter Home Goods website and its private Pinterest publishing application. Updated November 6, 2026.

Who we are

Lighter Home Goods publishes household product guides and practical ideas for reducing everyday waste at lighterhomegoods.com. Contact us at whatisthetruthrudy@gmail.com about this policy or your information.

Information you send us

Our contact and purchase-request forms collect the information you enter, including name, email, product reference, requested alternative, delivery state and ZIP, quantity and preferences. When you submit a request, FormSubmit processes the form and forwards a notification to our business email. Do not include payment details or a street address at the request stage.

Website hosting and external resources

The site is hosted using GitHub Pages with Cloudflare services. Hosting and resource providers may process your IP address, browser details, and request information to deliver and secure the site. Some pages request photographs from Unsplash and styling resources from Tailwind CSS or Cloudflare. These providers have their own privacy policies. This website has no customer accounts or checkout and currently has no site analytics integration.

Amazon affiliate links

Our site contains Amazon affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. Links include an affiliate identifier so Amazon can attribute qualifying purchases. After you follow a retailer link, the retailer controls its own cookies, purchases, and processing under its privacy policy. We do not receive your payment information through our website.

Pinterest publishing application

Our application is an internal tool for the owner of Lighter Home Goods to prepare and publish Pins to our own Pinterest account. It is not offered as a service to the public. Public website titles, descriptions, destination links, and illustrations are used to create draft Pins.

When authorized by the owner, the app requests permission to read boards, read Pins, and create Pins. It uses Pinterest board identifiers to select a destination and records published Pin identifiers to avoid duplicate posting. Authorization tokens and app credentials are stored locally on the owner's computer. The local draft queue contains Pin content, scheduling information, image paths, publication status, and errors. The app sends Pin text, image data, destination links, and the selected board identifier to Pinterest when publishing is enabled. Pinterest processes that information under its own privacy policy. The app does not collect Pinterest passwords, request private messages, or sell Pinterest account information.

Use, sharing, and retention

We use contact and purchase-request information to respond, check supplier availability, prepare delivery quotes and resolve requests. FormSubmit processes submitted forms, and our email provider handles the resulting notifications. Preparing a request does not collect payment or reserve stock.

Choices and deletion requests

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent us, subject to applicable recordkeeping obligations. The owner can revoke the app's access through Pinterest account settings and remove local tokens and credentials. Removing local draft records does not delete Pins already published on Pinterest; those must be managed on Pinterest. We use reasonable safeguards, including restricting access to account credentials, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy when our services or processing change. For questions or requests, email whatisthetruthrudy@gmail.com.
