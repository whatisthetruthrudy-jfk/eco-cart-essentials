Privacy and contact information

This page describes the current EcoCart Essentials website setup. Updated September 30, 2026.

Messages you send

The contact form asks for your name, email, subject, and message. These details are sent through FormSubmit to whatisthetruthrudy@gmail.com so EcoCart can respond. You can also send email directly. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive personal information.

Third-party services

FormSubmit processes contact-form submissions. Some pages load photographs from Unsplash and styling resources from Tailwind CSS and Cloudflare. Those providers receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address and browser details when your browser requests their resources. Their own policies govern their processing.

Retailer links

Shopping links are being prepared. When you follow an external retailer link, the retailer’s privacy and cookie policies apply. Affiliate links may use tracking identifiers to attribute qualifying purchases when the program is active.

Site features

This version of EcoCart has no customer accounts, checkout, or site analytics integration. Hosting providers may keep access logs when the site is published. This page will need to reflect any services added during publication.

Questions and requests

For questions about information you sent through the contact form, email whatisthetruthrudy@gmail.com. Avoid including sensitive information in your request.
