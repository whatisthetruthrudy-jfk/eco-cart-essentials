Reducing Waste Before You Recycle

By EcoCart Essentials · September 30, 2026

Reduce unnecessary purchases and reuse workable items first. For waste that remains, follow your local recycling program's accepted-material list; an item's packaging or recycling symbol is not a guarantee of local acceptance.

What can you avoid creating?

Choose quantities you can use and avoid duplicate tools. Before buying an organizer, sort what needs organizing. Before replacing a container, check whether a clean container you already own serves the same purpose.

Reuse with a clear purpose

Keep items you expect to use, rather than storing everything indefinitely. Reuse sturdy shopping bags and suitable containers. Donate usable items through an organization that accepts them; check its requirements first.

How should you sort recyclables?

Your collection provider's instructions are the deciding reference. Check its rules for material types, preparation, lids, bags, and special drop-off items. Do not rely on a general online list when your local provider gives different instructions.

Make the system easy to follow

Put a short accepted-items list near your recycling container. Set aside uncertain items until you can check. A small, clear system is easier for everyone in the household to use than several unlabeled bins.

Further reading

For local material sorting, consult your collection provider. The EPA guide to common recyclables provides additional background.
