What to check before choosing eco kitchen products

  • Check food-contact suitability and the manufacturer’s care instructions.
  • Measure cupboard and worktop space before choosing containers or tools.
  • Use the utensils and storage you already own before buying replacements.

These are selected product ideas, not hands-on reviews. Pictures are illustrative; check Amazon for the exact product, current availability, and care instructions.
