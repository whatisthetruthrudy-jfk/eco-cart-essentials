Eco Cleaning

Explore reusable cleaning tools, refill options, and practical buying notes. Start with supplies you already own, choose products for a specific cleaning job, and check materials and disposal instructions.

What to check before choosing eco cleaning products

  • Check materials, mop compatibility, bottle capacity, and the exact cleaner instructions.
  • Check surface suitability and local acceptance before putting bags or sponge parts in compost.
  • Consider whether an item you already own can do the same job.

These are selected product ideas, not hands-on reviews. Pictures are illustrative; check Amazon for exact specifications, current availability, and care instructions.
