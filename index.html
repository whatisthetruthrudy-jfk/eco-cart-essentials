Simple answers for a more eco-friendly home

How can I make my home more eco-friendly?

Start by using what you already own, repairing useful items, and identifying the disposable products you use most often. Replace those items with durable, reusable alternatives when needed. Explore our sustainable living guides for practical next steps.

How can I reduce plastic waste in my kitchen?

Reuse containers you already have, carry reusable shopping bags, and choose refill options when they suit your routine. If you need replacements, compare durable food-storage containers and washable cloths in our kitchen category. Check product materials and care instructions before buying.

How do you assess eco-friendly household products?

We look at usefulness, durability, reusability, materials, care requirements, and the evidence behind environmental claims. No product is impact-free, and buying less can be the better choice. Read our editorial policy to understand our approach.

What is Lighter Home Goods?

Lighter Home Goods is a browsing and information website with reusable product ideas and practical guides. It is not a retailer and does not process product orders.

Does reusable always mean more sustainable?

No. Materials, production, transport, cleaning, disposal, and how often you use an item all matter. Start by using what you already own and choose replacements for a specific need.
